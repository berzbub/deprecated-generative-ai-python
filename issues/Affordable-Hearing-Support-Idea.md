### Idea: Low-Cost Hearing Support from Everyday Earphones

For Mother's Day, we want to explore a practical gift for older adults: converting low-cost earphones into a basic hearing-support solution.

#### Problem
- Hearing aids are often too expensive for many families.
- Some people already have cheap or older earphones available.

#### Goal
- Provide guidance for using inexpensive earphones as an assistive listening option.
- Keep the approach affordable and easy to try.

#### Important safety note
- This is not a replacement for a certified medical hearing aid.
- Encourage users to seek professional hearing care when possible.
